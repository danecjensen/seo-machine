Ordering custom merch from Banner Goods happens in three steps, and you'll see a proof of exactly what you're getting before anything is made.

{% include work-strip.html label1="a logo proof on a cap" label2="products laid out for approval" label3="a finished order ready to ship" %}

## 1. Share your idea

[Start a project](/signup/) and tell us what you have in mind. When you're ready to order, send over the product, an approximate quantity, your colors and the date you need it all by, plus your logo or artwork if you have it. More detail means a more accurate quote.

## 2. Review your quote and proof

You'll get pricing for the order along with a digital proof: a mockup of your logo on the product that shows size, placement and colors. Request changes until it's right. Nothing is made until you approve.

## 3. We make it and ship it

Once the proof is approved, your order goes into production and ships to the address you choose. Working toward a fixed date, like an event or a launch? Mention it at the start so the schedule works around it.

## Tips for a smooth order

- **Give yourself time.** Everything is made to order, so leave room for proofing and production before your date.
- **Send the best artwork you have.** Vector files keep fine details crisp, especially for embroidery.
- **Think about fit.** For hats and socks, consider who'll wear them and whether one size covers everyone.
- **Bundle it.** Need hats and totes for the same event? Ask for both in one request and get one quote.
