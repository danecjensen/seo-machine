A custom blanket is premium merch that people hold on to. It ends up on the couch, in the car or wrapped around someone at a game, with your logo front and center for years.

## Designing your blanket

A blanket gives you space to work with: a full design, a repeating pattern of your mark, a mascot, or a message for a milestone. You'll see a proof before anything is made.

## Why blankets make lasting gifts

Most giveaways get used once. A blanket comes out every cold night, and people remember who gave it to them. That makes it the right pick when you want a gift that feels personal.
