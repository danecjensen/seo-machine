A hat is one of the most-worn pieces of branded merch you can give. A good cap becomes the one people grab on the way out the door, season after season, with your logo right up front.

## Choosing how your logo goes on

Embroidery is the classic choice for hats: it looks crisp, lasts and survives the wash. Logos with very fine lines or lots of colors can work better as a woven or printed patch. Share your logo and we'll recommend what will look best on the style you choose.

## Colors and fit

Match the hat to your brand colors, or split an order across colors for different teams or departments. Most caps have a strap or snapback, so one size fits most adults.
