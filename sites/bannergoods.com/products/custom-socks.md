Custom socks are the merch people don't expect, and that's why they work. Everyone wears socks, they fit nearly everyone, and a pair in your colors starts conversations. They're light and pack flat too, so they're easy to mail to a remote team or add to a welcome box.

## How the design is made

Most custom socks are knit, so your logo and colors are part of the sock itself instead of printed on the surface. Knit designs stay bright through countless washes. Designs with photos or very fine detail can be printed instead. Share your artwork and we'll suggest the right method.

## Designing your socks

Use your logo, a repeating pattern, team colors or a mascot. Add a message on the sole or a detail on the cuff. You'll approve a mockup before production starts.
