People use a good tote bag constantly. It goes to the store, the gym, the beach and the office, and your logo goes along every time.

## How your logo is printed

Screen printing is the standard for totes: bold, durable and ideal for logos in one or two colors. Full-color artwork or photos can use other print methods. Send your artwork and we'll recommend the right approach for your bag.

## Size and color

A natural canvas bag shows off a dark logo, while a colored bag with a light print stands out in a crowd. Think about what goes inside: a laptop and notebook at a conference, or groceries at the farmers market. A tote can also be the packaging for the rest of your merch, turning a pile of items into a welcome kit.
