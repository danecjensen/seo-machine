## Picking the right product

Start with how people will use it, then pick the product that fits.

- **Hats** get worn constantly. They suit staff, teams and outdoor events.
- **Tote bags** go everywhere: groceries, the gym, the office. They're great for conferences, retail and welcome kits, and they can hold the rest of your merch.
- **Blankets** are gifts people keep for years. Use them for clients, donors, milestones and thank-yous.
- **Socks** are fun, fit nearly everyone and ship flat, which makes them easy to send to remote teams.

Still deciding? Every order starts with a digital proof, so you'll see your logo on the product before anything is made.
