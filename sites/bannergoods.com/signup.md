## What you'll get

- **Product ideas**: which hats, totes, blankets and socks work best with a logo, and what each one is good for.
- **Design tips**: how to make your logo look sharp embroidered, printed, woven or knit.
- **A next step**: when you're ready to make something, reply to any email and tell us about it.

## Who it's for

Anyone thinking about branded merch: companies planning staff or client gifts, teams and schools after matching gear, nonprofits raising money with merch, and organizers who want giveaways that last. You don't need a finished logo or a budget to sign up. A rough idea is plenty: tell us the occasion, about how many people you're making things for and when you need them, and we'll suggest products that fit. If you're comparing options for a first order, our emails cover what each product is good at and what to expect from proofs and production, so you can plan with confidence.

## Your email stays with us

We only use your email to send Banner Goods updates, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Choosing a product is optional, but it helps us send ideas that fit. Not sure yet? Pick "Something else" or leave it blank.
