Happy Club makes custom promotional products that people keep using long after the event is over. Instead of another pen or stress ball that ends up in a drawer, give your customers, staff or fans something they'll wear, carry or curl up with, and see your logo out in the world every day.

## What we make

- **[Custom hats](/products/custom-hats/)**: caps, dad hats, trucker hats and beanies with an embroidered or printed logo.
- **[Custom tote bags](/products/custom-tote-bags/)**: canvas and cotton totes for conferences, retail and giveaways.
- **[Custom blankets](/products/custom-blankets/)**: woven and fleece blankets for gifts, fundraisers and premium swag.
- **[Custom socks](/products/custom-socks/)**: socks with your colors and logo, for teams, staff and events.

## Who orders from us

Companies use our custom wearables for employee welcome kits, client gifts and trade show giveaways. Schools, clubs and sports teams order matching gear for players and fans. Nonprofits sell branded blankets and totes to raise money, and event organizers hand them out so people remember the day.

## How ordering works

1. **Tell us what you need.** Pick a product, a rough quantity and the date you need it by, and send your logo.
2. **See a proof.** We'll come back with pricing and a digital mockup showing your logo on the product.
3. **Approve and relax.** Once you sign off on the proof, we make your order and ship it to you.

Read more about the process on [how it works](/how-it-works/), or [sign up for ideas by email](/signup/) and tell us what you'd like to make.

## Why promotional wearables work

Useful items stay in use. A hat goes to the ballgame, a tote goes to the grocery store, a blanket goes on the couch and socks go everywhere. Each time, your brand comes along. That's more impressions for the same budget than a giveaway that gets thrown away, and it tells people you cared enough to give them something good.
