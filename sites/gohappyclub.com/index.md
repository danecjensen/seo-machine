Happy Club makes custom promotional products that people keep using long after the event is over. Instead of another pen or stress ball that ends up in a drawer, give your customers, staff or fans something they'll wear, carry or curl up with, and see your logo out in the world every day.

## Why promotional wearables work

Useful items stay in use. A hat goes to the ballgame, a tote goes to the grocery store, a blanket goes on the couch and socks go everywhere. Each time, your brand comes along. That's more impressions for the same budget than a giveaway that gets thrown away, and it tells people you cared enough to give them something good.
