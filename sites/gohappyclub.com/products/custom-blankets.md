A custom blanket is a premium promotional product that people keep for years. It's the kind of gift that ends up on a couch or in a car, wrapped around someone at a game or a campfire, with your logo front and center.

## Designing your blanket

A blanket has plenty of room, so you can go beyond a small logo: a full design, a pattern of your mark, a mascot, or a message for an anniversary or milestone. We'll send a proof before anything is made so you can see exactly how it will look.

## Why blankets make memorable gifts

Most promotional items are used for a moment. A blanket gets used every cold evening, and people tend to remember who gave it to them. That makes it a good choice when you want a gift that feels personal rather than promotional.
