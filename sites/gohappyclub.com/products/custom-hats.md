Custom hats are one of the most worn promotional products there is. A good cap gets picked up on the way out the door for years, which makes it one of the best places to put your logo.

## Logo options

Embroidery is the most common way to add a logo to a hat, because it looks sharp, lasts and stands up to washing. Some designs suit a printed or woven patch instead, especially logos with fine detail or many colors. Tell us about your logo and we'll suggest the option that will look best on the style you pick.

## Colors and sizing

Pick a hat color that matches your brand, or mix colors across an order for different teams or departments. Most caps have an adjustable strap or snapback, so one size fits most adults.
