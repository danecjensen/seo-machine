Custom socks are a fun, unexpected promotional product. Everyone wears them, they fit almost everyone, and a pair in your brand colors gets people talking.

## Sock styles

- **Crew socks**: the most popular height, with the most room for your design.
- **Ankle and low-cut socks**: for athletic and summer wear.
- **Athletic socks**: cushioned and built for teams, races and gym gear.
- **Knee-high socks**: for sports uniforms and bold designs.

## How the design is made

Many custom socks are knit, which means your logo and colors are woven into the sock itself instead of printed on top. Knit designs stay bright wash after wash. Some designs with photos or fine detail suit printed socks instead. Share your artwork and we'll tell you which approach fits.

## Designing your socks

Use your logo, a repeating pattern, your team colors or your mascot. Add a message on the sole or a design on the cuff. We'll send a mockup for you to approve before production starts.

## Good uses for custom socks

Race and fun-run swag, team uniforms, employee gifts, trade show giveaways that stand out, school spirit gear, and merch for bands, brands and creators. Socks also pack flat and weigh very little, which makes them easy to mail to remote teams or tuck into a welcome box.
