Custom tote bags are a giveaway people genuinely use. They go to the grocery store, the gym, the beach and the office, carrying your logo with them every time.

## Printing your logo

Screen printing is the usual choice for tote bags: bold, durable and well suited to one or two colored logos. Designs with many colors or photos can be printed with other methods. Send us your artwork and we'll recommend what will look best on the bag you choose.

## Picking a size and color

Natural canvas shows off a dark logo, while a colored bag with a light print makes a brand stand out. Think about what people will carry in it: a laptop and notebook at a conference, or groceries and a water bottle at a farmers market. A tote can also hold the rest of your swag, so it works as the packaging for a whole welcome kit or event bundle.
