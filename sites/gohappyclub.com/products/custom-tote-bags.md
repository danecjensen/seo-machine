Custom tote bags are a giveaway people genuinely use. They go to the grocery store, the gym, the beach and the office, carrying your logo with them every time.

## Tote bag styles

- **Cotton totes**: lightweight and affordable, a good fit for conference bags and large giveaways.
- **Canvas totes**: heavier and sturdier, for retail packaging, gifts and merch people will pay for.
- **Totes with gussets or pockets**: extra room and structure for bigger loads.

## Printing your logo

Screen printing is the usual choice for tote bags: bold, durable and well suited to one or two colored logos. Designs with many colors or photos can be printed with other methods. Send us your artwork and we'll recommend what will look best on the bag you choose.

## Picking a size and color

Natural canvas shows off a dark logo, while a colored bag with a light print makes a brand stand out. Think about what people will carry in it: a laptop and notebook at a conference, or groceries and a water bottle at a farmers market.

## Good uses for custom totes

Conference and event bags, welcome kits for new employees, retail shopping bags, fundraising merch, wedding and party favors, and client gift bags. A tote can also hold the rest of your swag, so it works as the packaging for a whole welcome kit or event bundle.
