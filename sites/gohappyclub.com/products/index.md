## Choosing the right custom product

Each of our products is used in a different way, so start with how you want people to use it.

- **Hats** are one of the most worn promotional products there is. Pick them for staff, teams and events where people will be outdoors or on the move.
- **Tote bags** get carried to the grocery store, the gym and the office. They suit conferences, retail and welcome kits, and can hold the rest of your swag.
- **Blankets** are premium gifts that people keep for years. Choose them for clients, donors, anniversaries and thank-yous.
- **Socks** are fun and unexpected, fit almost everyone and mail flat, which makes them easy to send to remote teams.

Not sure yet? Every order starts with a digital proof, so you can see your logo on the product before anything is made.
