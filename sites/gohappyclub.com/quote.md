Tell us what you'd like to make and we'll reply with a quote and a digital proof of your logo on the product. A quote is free and there's no obligation to order.

{% include quote-form.html %}

## What to include

- **The product**: hats, tote bags, blankets, socks, or a mix.
- **Quantity**: a rough number is fine, and you can ask for prices at a few quantities.
- **Colors and sizes**: the product colors you want and, for hats and socks, the sizes.
- **Your logo**: a high-resolution or vector file gives the best results.
- **Your deadline**: the date you need everything in hand, especially if it's for an event.

## What happens next

We'll review your request and reply by email with pricing and a mockup. You can ask for changes to the design, the product or the quantity before you decide. Nothing is made until you approve the proof.

## Ordering for an event?

If your order is for a conference, a game, a fundraiser or a company party, put the event date in your request. We'll let you know what's realistic for that date, and suggest products that suit the occasion and your budget. Ordering several products for the same event? Include them all in one request and we'll quote them together, so everything arrives in time.

Not sure which product fits? Just describe the event and the people you want to reach, and we'll suggest a few options.
