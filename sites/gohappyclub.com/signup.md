## What you'll get

- **Product ideas**: hats, totes, blankets and socks that work well with a logo, and what each suits best.
- **Design tips**: how to get your logo looking sharp when it's embroidered, printed, woven or knit.
- **A way to start**: when you're ready to make something, reply to any email and tell us about it.

## Who it's for

The list is for anyone thinking about custom branded products: businesses planning employee or client gifts, teams and schools after matching gear, nonprofits looking for fundraising merch, and event organizers who want giveaways people keep. You don't need a finished logo or a set budget to sign up, just an idea of what you might want to make.

## Your email stays with us

We only use your email to send you Happy Club updates, and you can unsubscribe from any email with one click. Picking a product is optional, but it helps us send you ideas that fit what you want to make. If you're not sure yet, pick "Something else" or leave it blank, and we'll send you a mix of ideas across every product we make.
