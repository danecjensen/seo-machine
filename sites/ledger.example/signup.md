Your free trial of Ledgerly lasts 30 days and includes every Pro feature. You don't need a card to start, and nothing is charged when the trial ends unless you choose a plan.

## What happens next

1. Create your account with an email address and a password, or sign in with Google.
2. Connect the bank account and cards you use for business. Connections are read-only.
3. Ledgerly imports up to 12 months of history and sorts it into categories.
4. Spend five minutes reviewing anything flagged, and your books are up to date.

## What you will see on day one

Your dashboard shows profit so far this year, your estimated quarterly tax payment and the date it is due, unpaid invoices, and the categories where you spend the most. You can create your first invoice right away and send it to a client from the same screen.

## Questions before you start?

Most people get set up in under ten minutes. If you're moving from a spreadsheet or another tool, you can import a CSV of past transactions and Ledgerly will categorize those too. Our support team answers email within one business day during the trial, and we're happy to help you import your old data or set up rules for tricky accounts.

When you're ready to choose a plan, compare them on the [pricing page](/pricing/). Not sure Ledgerly does what you need? Read through the [features](/features/) or browse our [guides](/blog/) on freelance bookkeeping and taxes.
