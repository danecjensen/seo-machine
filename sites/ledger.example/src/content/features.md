Ledgerly combines a freelancer expense tracker, invoicing and tax planning in one app. Here is everything included in every plan.

## Expense tracking

Ledgerly pulls transactions from your bank and cards every night. Each one is matched to a category such as software, travel or home office, using the same categories as Schedule C. When you fix a category once, Ledgerly writes a rule so you never fix it again. Mixed personal and business account? Mark personal transactions with one tap and they disappear from your reports.

## Receipt capture

Snap a photo of a receipt or forward an email receipt to your private Ledgerly address. We read the merchant, date and amount and attach the receipt to the matching transaction. Receipts are stored for seven years, which covers the IRS record-keeping window.

## Invoicing

Create an invoice in under a minute from a saved client and rate. Clients can pay by card or bank transfer, and the payment is matched to the invoice automatically. Late invoices get a polite reminder on the schedule you choose.

## Quarterly estimated taxes

Ledgerly calculates your federal estimated tax payments using your actual profit so far, then shows the amount and the due date for each quarter. You can see the effect of a large expense before you make it. State estimates are available in 41 states.

## Reports and accountant access

Run a profit and loss statement, an expense breakdown or a 1099 income summary for any date range. Invite your accountant with read-only access so they can pull what they need without asking you for spreadsheets.

## Security

Bank connections are read-only. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and two-factor sign-in is on by default.
