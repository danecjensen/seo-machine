Ledgerly is bookkeeping for freelancers who would rather be doing paid work. Most bookkeeping tools were built for companies with an accountant on staff. Ledgerly is built for one person with a laptop, a handful of clients and a bank account that mixes business and life.

## Why freelancers switch to Ledgerly

- **Automatic categories.** Every transaction from your bank or card lands in the right category, using rules that learn from the corrections you make.
- **Tax money, set aside.** Ledgerly estimates your quarterly taxes as you earn and tells you exactly how much to move to savings.
- **Invoices that get paid.** Send an invoice from the same place you track it, with card and bank payments built in.
- **Clean books at year end.** Export a profit and loss report or hand your accountant read-only access in one click.

## How it works

1. Connect your business bank account and cards. Ledgerly supports more than 10,000 US banks.
2. Review the week's transactions in about five minutes. Approve, recategorize or split anything that looks off.
3. Check your tax dashboard before each quarterly deadline and pay what it recommends.

That's it. No double-entry jargon, no chart of accounts to design, no spreadsheet to keep in sync.

## Built for the way freelancers earn

Freelance income is lumpy. A big project lands in March and nothing arrives in April. Ledgerly smooths that out by showing a running estimate of your annual income, your effective tax rate and what you can safely pay yourself this month. When a client pays late, you will see it on the dashboard before it becomes a cash crunch.
