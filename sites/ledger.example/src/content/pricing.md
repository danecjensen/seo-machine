## Which plan is right for you?

Choose **Solo** if you have one business account, invoice a few clients a month and file a simple Schedule C. Most freelancers start here.

Choose **Pro** if you have several accounts or cards, drive for work, owe state estimated taxes, or work with an accountant who needs their own login.

## What you get on both plans

Both plans include automatic categorization, rules that learn from your corrections, receipt storage for seven years, profit and loss reports, 1099 income summaries and read-only bank connections. Support by email answers within one business day on Solo and within four hours on Pro.
