Northside Plumbing started in 2009 with one truck and one plumber, Luis Ortega, who had spent twelve years working for larger companies and wanted to run things differently: honest quotes, on-time arrivals and work he would be proud to show his own family.

Today we're a team of nine, including six licensed plumbers, two dispatchers and an office manager who answers the phone herself. We're still family-run, and Luis still takes calls on Saturdays.

## Licensed and insured

Every Northside plumber holds a license from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and we're happy to send a certificate before we start any job.

## How we work

We quote a fixed price for the job before we begin, based on what we find when we look, not a guess over the phone. If something changes once a wall or floor is open, we stop and talk to you before going further. We pull permits where the City of Austin requires them, and we schedule the inspection for you.

## Our guarantee

Labor is guaranteed for two years. If something we fixed fails in that time, we come back and fix it at no charge. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we handle the warranty claim so you don't have to.

## In the community

We sponsor the Wells Branch youth soccer league and donate a water heater installation each winter to a family referred by a local food bank. We hire apprentices from Austin Community College's plumbing program, and several of our licensed plumbers started with us that way.
