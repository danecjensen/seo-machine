The fastest way to book a plumber is to call us at [(512) 555-0142](tel:+15125550142). A real person answers 7 days a week from 7am to 7pm. After hours, leave a message and we'll call back first thing in the morning, or press 1 for emergencies.

## What to have ready

- Your address and the best phone number to reach you.
- A short description of the problem, and when it started.
- For water heaters, the brand and the size printed on the label, if you can see it.
- Any times you can't be home. We give a two-hour arrival window and text when we're on the way.

## Our hours

Monday through Sunday, 7am to 7pm. Emergency service is available around the clock for burst pipes, sewer backups and gas water heater problems.

## Where we are

Our shop is at 1200 W Braker Ln, Austin, TX 78758. We don't take walk-in repairs, but you're welcome to pick up parts or drop off paperwork during business hours.

## What happens when we arrive

Your plumber will introduce themselves, look at the problem and give you a fixed price before starting. You can approve it, ask questions, or decide to wait. Once the work is done, we test everything with you and clean up. You'll receive an emailed invoice with the details of the work and the warranty.
