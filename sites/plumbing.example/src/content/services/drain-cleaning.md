Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Our drain cleaning service clears clogs in kitchen sinks, bathroom sinks, showers, tubs, toilets and the main sewer line, and we use a camera to show you exactly what caused the problem.

## Signs of a clog

- Water drains slowly or backs up into the tub or shower.
- Gurgling sounds from drains or the toilet.
- A sewer smell near floor drains or outside cleanouts.
- More than one fixture is slow at the same time, which usually points to the main line.

## How we clear drains

For single fixtures we use a hand or machine auger sized to the pipe. For main lines and repeat clogs, we run a camera first, then clear the line with hydro-jetting, which scours grease and roots off the pipe walls instead of punching a hole through them.

## Tree roots and older homes

Many North Austin homes built before 1985 have clay or cast iron sewer lines that roots can get into. If the camera shows cracked or separated pipe, we'll show you the footage and price out a repair or a trenchless liner, with no pressure to decide that day.

## Preventing clogs

Keep grease out of the kitchen sink, use a hair catcher in showers, and only flush toilet paper. If you've had more than two clogs in a year, a yearly hydro-jetting visit usually costs less than repeated emergency calls. [Book drain cleaning](/contact/) online or call us.
