No hot water, rusty water or a puddle under the tank? Our water heater repair service covers gas and electric tank heaters and tankless units from every major brand. Most repairs are finished in a single visit, because our trucks carry the common thermostats, elements, igniters and valves.

## Signs you need water heater repair

- Hot water runs out much faster than it used to.
- Water is rusty or smells like rotten eggs.
- The tank pops, rumbles or bangs while heating.
- There's water pooling around the base.
- The pilot light on a gas heater won't stay lit.

## What a repair costs

Most water heater repairs cost between $149 and $450, including parts and labor. We quote a fixed price after we look at the unit, before any work starts.

## Replacement

When a tank leaks, repair isn't possible and replacement is the safe option. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, high-efficiency heat pump units and tankless heaters, usually the same day. We haul the old unit away and pull the City of Austin permit.

## Tankless water heaters

Tankless units need descaling every year in Austin because of our hard water. We service Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem tankless heaters, and we can tell you honestly whether a tankless upgrade makes sense for your household.

## Keep it running longer

Flush your tank once a year to clear sediment, and test the pressure relief valve. We can do both during a [maintenance visit](/contact/) for $99.
